Coffer is a personal budgeting app that actually understands how real life works: your rent hits every month, your car insurance hits once a year, and your side-hustle income doesn't look the same two months in a row. Instead of forcing all of that into a flat spreadsheet, Coffer keeps it organized automatically — so you always know exactly where you stand, this month and every month.

No spreadsheets. No formulas to break. No guessing. Just a clear picture of your money, wherever you are.

---

## ✨ Features

### 💰 Effortless Income & Expense Tracking
Add your income sources and expenses once, and let Coffer do the math. Mark something as **recurring** (rent, subscriptions, your salary) or **one-off** (a new laptop, a birthday gift), and Coffer automatically works out what belongs in any given month — no manual re-entry, ever.

### 📊 A Dashboard That Actually Makes Sense
See your net income, monthly spending, remaining budget, and savings rate at a glance. A simple color-coded bar shows how much of your budget you've used, and a spending breakdown chart shows exactly where your money is going, category by category.

### 🎯 Goals & Debt Payoff Planning
Set a savings or investment goal and watch your progress fill in as you go. Tracking a loan or credit card? Coffer's built-in payoff calculator shows you exactly how long it'll take to clear — or flip it around and ask "how much do I need to pay each month to be debt-free by a certain date?"

### 🗂️ Categories That Work Your Way
Organize spending into categories with their own colors and icons. Rename them, recolor them, add your own, or restore the originals — it's your budget, your rules.

### 📈 Reports You Can Actually Share
Get a month-over-month and year-over-year view of your spending trends, plus a full breakdown of your income and expenses — and export either one as a polished PDF in one tap.

### 📥 Your Data, Your Way
Import your existing spreadsheet data via CSV, export anything back out the same way, or download a complete backup of your entire budget as a single file whenever you want. Nothing is ever locked in.

### 🔔 Helpful Nudges, Not Nagging
Turn on gentle notifications for bills about to end, categories running over budget, or goals you've just hit — entirely optional, and entirely under your control.

### 📱 Install It Like a Real App
Add Coffer to your home screen on your phone or desktop and it behaves like a native app — its own icon, its own window, and it still opens even when your connection drops.

### 🌗 Looks Great, Day or Night
A clean, modern dark theme by default, with a light theme available whenever you want it.

## 🛠 How It Works

Using Coffer is simple, and it's designed to stay that way:

1. **Tell Coffer what you earn and spend.** Add your income sources and your expenses, marking each one as recurring or one-off.
2. **Coffer does the thinking.** It automatically works out what actually applies to any given month — a yearly bill only counts the month it's due, a monthly bill counts every month, and so on.
3. **Everything updates instantly.** Your dashboard, charts, budgets, and goals all reflect your latest numbers the moment you make a change — nothing to recalculate or refresh.
4. **Your data stays yours.** Everything is stored securely and privately under your own account, viewable only by you. Export it, back it up, or take it with you at any time.

No manual formulas. No monthly "reset the spreadsheet" ritual. Just add what's true about your money, and let Coffer handle the rest.

## ❓ FAQ

**Is Coffer free to use?**
Yes.

**Do I need to create a new account?**
No — Coffer uses Google Sign-In, so if you already have a Google account, you're ready to go.

**Is my financial data private?**
Yes. Your income, expenses, and settings are visible only to you. Nobody else — including other users — can see your data.

**Does Coffer connect to my bank account?**
No. Coffer doesn't link to your bank — you add your income and expenses yourself. This is intentional: it keeps your data entirely under your control, with no third party ever needing access to your bank credentials.

**Can I use Coffer on my phone?**
Yes — Coffer is designed mobile-first, and you can install it to your home screen so it behaves like a native app.

**What happens if I lose my internet connection?**
Coffer will still open and show you whatever you've already loaded. New changes will sync again once you're back online.

**Can I get my data out if I ever want to leave?**
Yes, any time — export your expenses or income as a CSV, or download a complete backup of everything as a single file, right from Settings.

**Do you support currencies other than my own?**
Coffer currently supports USD, GBP, and EUR, with more planned — see the [Roadmap](#-roadmap) below.

**I found a bug or have an idea — where do I send it?**
See [Support](#-support) below.
